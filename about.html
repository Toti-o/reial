About Reial

A production partner, from brief to delivery.

Reial Production is a Kenya-based commercial video production company working with brands, organisations and agencies across East Africa.

End-to-end execution

From concept and scripting through filming, editing, graphics and final delivery one team, one process, end to end.

Content built for use

Assets delivered in the formats your organisation actually needs social, web, presentations, campaigns, internal comms.

Reliable collaboration

Flexible capability

Our approach

From brief to final delivery.

Every project follows the same clear process, so you always know what is happening and when.

01

Discover

We clarify your objective, audience, message, deliverables, timeline and success criteria.

02

Develop

We shape the concept, script, interview questions, visual direction, locations and production plan.

03

Produce

We manage the shoot, crew, equipment, direction, interviews, lighting, sound and visual capture.

04

Finish

We edit, colour-correct, mix sound, add graphics and captions, create cutdowns and prepare final files.

05

Deliver

You receive organised, platform-ready assets in the agreed formats.
