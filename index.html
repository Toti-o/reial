How it works

From brief to final delivery.

A structured process that keeps the work on track, the communication clear and the final assets ready to use.

01

Discover

We clarify your objective, audience, message, deliverables, timeline and success criteria.

02

Develop

We shape the concept, script, interview questions, visual direction, locations and production plan.

03

Produce

04

Finish

Deliver

Ongoing partnerships

Consistent content for organisations that are always communicating.

Many organisations do not need one video. They need a reliable flow of content throughout the month. Reial supports ongoing production partnerships built around your campaigns, product calendar, events and communication priorities.

Discuss a partnership
  • Monthly content planning
  • Scheduled filming sessions
  • Promotional videos
  • Social media cutdowns
  • Branded graphics
  • Interviews and testimonials
  • Event and campaign coverage
  • Priority production support
  • Platform-ready delivery

Partnerships are scoped around the number of videos, filming days, crew size, locations, graphics, revisions, turnaround time and any posting or management requirements.
